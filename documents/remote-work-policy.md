# Remote Work Policy (2026)

## Purpose
This policy sets out the conditions under which employees may work remotely and the responsibilities that apply to remote work arrangements.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probationary period are eligible to apply for remote work of up to three days per week. Approval is at the discretion of the employee's manager and depends on the nature of the role and business requirements. Roles that require in-person presence, such as front-desk and laboratory roles, may not be eligible.

## Equipment and Facilities
The organisation provides each approved remote worker with a laptop and one external monitor for use at home. Employees are responsible for providing a reliable internet connection at their own cost; the organisation does not reimburse home internet or electricity expenses. Any equipment provided remains the property of the organisation and must be returned when the arrangement ends.

## Information Security
Remote workers must connect through the organisation's Virtual Private Network (VPN) at all times when accessing internal systems. Confidential or sensitive documents must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Screens must be locked when the workstation is unattended.

## Availability and Hours
Remote employees must be contactable during the core hours of 10:00am to 3:00pm. Any variation to an employee's regular working hours must be agreed with their manager in advance. Remote workers are expected to attend the office for team meetings and events when reasonably requested.

## Review
Remote work arrangements are reviewed every six months and may be varied or withdrawn where business needs change or where the arrangement is not working effectively.
