# Leave and Expenses Policy (2026)

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave should be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance where possible. Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year up to a maximum of 10 days.

## Personal and Carer's Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid personal (sick) and carer's leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than two consecutive days.

## Volunteer Leave
Employees may take up to 3 days of paid volunteer leave per year to support approved community organisations. Requests must be approved by the employee's manager and the chosen organisation must be a registered charity or community group.

## Study Leave
Up to 5 days of paid study leave per year is available with manager approval, for courses relevant to the employee's role or professional development.

## Expense Reimbursement
Approved work-related expenses are reimbursed on the 15th of each month. Claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred and must include a valid receipt. Expenses over $500 require prior written approval from a manager. Personal expenses, fines, and alcohol are not reimbursable.

## Travel
Economy-class travel is the standard for domestic trips. Accommodation and reasonable meal costs are covered when travelling for work, subject to the daily limits published on the finance portal.
