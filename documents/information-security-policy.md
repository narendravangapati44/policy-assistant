# Information Security Policy (2026)

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access the organisation's systems, data, or devices.

## Passwords and Authentication
All accounts must use a unique password of at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for access to email, the finance portal, and any system holding personal information. Passwords must not be shared or reused across systems.

## Data Classification
Information is classified as Public, Internal, or Confidential. Confidential information — including personal data, financial records, and contracts — must only be stored in approved systems and must not be emailed to personal accounts or copied to unapproved cloud storage.

## Acceptable Use
Organisation devices are provided for work purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, breach the law, or introduce security risks. Installing unapproved software is prohibited.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident — including lost devices, phishing emails, or unauthorised access — must be reported to the IT Service Desk within one hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate a suspected breach yourself.

## Access Control
Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis. Access is reviewed quarterly, and is revoked immediately when an employee changes role or leaves the organisation.
